1. If you are using Windows it may occasionally be necessary to reset your USB driver before proceeding. See the Windows instructions at the end of this page.
2. After disconnecting your instrument from the power source, unscrew and remove the bottom plate in order to gain access to the internal circuit board.
3. Connect the USB port marked as "Step (1)" to your computer using a micro-USB data cable (a power-only cable won't work).
4. Find the two small buttons marked as "Step (2)". While pressing and holding the TOP button, press and release the BOTTOM button. <a href="https://daisy.audio/img/boot-reset_wide.gif" target=”_blank”>Like this</a>.
5. Click the "Connect" button below.
6. In the dialog box that opens, select "DFU in FS Mode" and then click "Connect".
7. Click the "Update" button below and wait for the progress bar to finish. You are done!
